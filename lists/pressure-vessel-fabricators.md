## What I'd flag

- A "read 20 rows" check found only about 50% of name-flagged rows were real fabricators. A name match overstates the pool.
- A fit-check pass on 9 leads: 3 clean, 1 fixable (company writes its name differently), 2 wrong (a reseller and an installer), 3 unverifiable. Roughly one lead in three needs a human decision.
- A trade directory listed 7,037 company names, but names only and many foreign, so not usable as a source.
- Stopped at the preview. A new campaign is a 10-lead preview and copy, then a send decision.
