# Free tools for cold email teams

Five tools I built and host for free. Each one runs entirely in your browser, so nothing you paste or upload leaves your computer. Use them right here, or open one full screen.

{% include tools-embed.html height=1100 %}

| Tool | What it does | Source |
|---|---|---|
| [Cold email checker](https://trav54219.github.io/cold-email-checker/) | Checks copy for spam phrases, AI-sounding lines, broken merge fields and spintax, length, links and reading grade. Previews random variants and converts spintax between Smartlead, Instantly and PlusVibe. | [repo](https://github.com/Trav54219/cold-email-checker) |
| [Domain health](https://trav54219.github.io/domain-health/) | Checks MX, SPF lookups, DKIM, DMARC, domain age and website for every sending domain at once, with the exact DNS record to add. Its CLI also checks blocklists and can alert Slack every morning. | [repo](https://github.com/Trav54219/domain-health) |
| [Lead name cleaner](https://trav54219.github.io/lead-name-cleaner/) | Upload a lead CSV and get it back with clean company and first names, plus a review flag on every row that isn't safe to merge. Tested on 798 real rows. | [repo](https://github.com/Trav54219/lead-name-cleaner) |
| [Reply rate checker](https://trav54219.github.io/reply-rate-checker/) | Takes out machine replies, puts rates on contacted leads, and runs Fisher's exact test to say whether an A/B winner is real or how many more sends you need. The reply classifier got 141 of 141 real replies right. | [repo](https://github.com/Trav54219/reply-rate-checker) |
| [Lead list scrubber](https://trav54219.github.io/lead-list-scrubber/) | Removes customers, past contacts, unsubscribes, duplicates, info@ addresses and anyone over N per company before upload. Every removed row gets a reason. | [repo](https://github.com/Trav54219/lead-list-scrubber) |
