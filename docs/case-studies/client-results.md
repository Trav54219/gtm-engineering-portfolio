# Client results

These are the client engagements behind the numbers on my resume. Each figure is stated the way the client's case study states it, with the time it took. Nothing here is rounded up or combined into a rate that wasn't measured.

Two kinds of engagement are mixed on this page, and they're measured differently. **Introduction work** is measured in qualified introductions delivered. **Lead generation work** is measured in the revenue or placements the client reported. A revenue number from one kind is never evidence for the other.

## Introductions: 15 across 3 engagements

| Client | Result | Time |
|---|---|---|
| Regent Peak Wealth Advisors | 6 qualified introductions | 45 days |
| Clean Seas West Virginia (plastics recycling plant) | 6 routed introductions | 120 days |
| Respilon | 3 qualified introductions | about 43 days |

Regent Peak's sourcing filter was the firm's existing ideal-client definition. Introductions were only opened where the fit was already there. Clean Seas is the slowest result and sets the honest expectation for heavy industrial markets: low volume, long cycles, and the value sits in a few correct conversations.

## Revenue and placements: $325K+ across 4 engagements

| Client | Result | Time |
|---|---|---|
| Connect Group | +$105K in additional revenue, from more qualified placements | one quarter |
| Vention | +$85K in additional revenue | one quarter |
| Crawford Thomas Recruiting | 39 candidate placements (about $100K) | one quarter |
| Hippocratic AI | 2 partnership deals worth $35,000 | 60 days |

$105K + $85K + about $100K + $35K is where the $325K+ comes from.
